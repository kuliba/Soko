Privacy Policy

Sokoban · Last updated: June 10, 2026

Overview

Sokoban is a puzzle game. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about our users. All data remains on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration or login
  • No tracking or analytics
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No camera or microphone access
  • No contacts or files

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is saved only on your device using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults and local files) and is never transmitted anywhere:

  • Game progress (completed levels, move history)
  • Settings (sound, animation speed, theme)
  • In-app purchase status (Premium)

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or handle your payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to all transactions: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published at this URL with a revised date.

Contact
